Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 10, 2026

HealthyMama is built around the idea that your pregnancy notes are yours. This page explains what stays on your device, what (if anything) leaves it, and what we will never do.

What stays on your device

Everything you create inside HealthyMama lives in your phone's private storage:

  • Journal entries, photos and voice notes
  • Daily mood check-ins
  • Nutrition logs from the food scanner
  • Your name, due date and stage
  • Apple Health data we read with your permission (read-only — we never write to Health)

We do not have a server that mirrors any of this. There is no HealthyMama account.

What can leave your device — only if you choose

  • Backups. When you tap Export full backup, a single file is generated for you to share or save (iCloud, AirDrop, Files). You decide where it goes.
  • PDF journal. When you tap Export Journal as PDF, the same applies.
  • AI nutrition. The food scanner first tries Apple Intelligence (on-device). If the device doesn't support it, the food name you typed is sent to a backend that proxies a third-party language model and returns nutrition estimates. The food name and the resulting estimates are not stored on the server.

What we never do

  • We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not run ads.
  • We do not sell, rent or trade any data.
  • We do not collect analytics, crash data or device identifiers.

Permissions we ask for

  • Camera — to take meal photos in the food scanner.
  • Photos — to attach a meal photo from your library.
  • Microphone — to record a voice note in the journal.
  • Notifications — for the morning check-in and reminders, only if you turn them on.
  • Apple Health — read-only access to steps, sleep and resting heart rate, only if you connect it.
  • Reminders — to mirror gentle nudges into a "HealthyMama" list, only if you turn it on. Only generic titles are written, never your journal text.

Children

HealthyMama is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has used the app and you'd like the app deleted from their device, simply uninstall it — no further action is needed because nothing is stored off-device.

Sign in with Apple

Sign in with Apple is optional. If you use it, Apple shares your name (only if you allow it). We store this on your device only.

Changes

If this policy changes meaningfully, we'll update the date above and note the change in the app's About screen.
