Frequently asked

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone, in HealthyMama's private storage. There is no central server holding your journal, photos or voice notes.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

On your old phone: Profile → Backup → Export full backup. Save the file to iCloud Drive or AirDrop it across.
On the new phone: install HealthyMama, on the welcome screen tap Restore from backup, pick the file. Everything comes back.

How do I delete my data?

Uninstall the app. Because nothing is stored off-device, that's it. If you've shared a backup file somewhere (iCloud, email), delete that too.

How do I cancel a subscription?

iOS Settings → your name at the top → Subscriptions → HealthyMama. You can also restore an existing purchase from the paywall.

The food scanner shows "Apple Intelligence Active" — what does that mean?

On supported iPhones, the AI runs locally on your device. The food name never leaves your phone in that case.

Apple Health says zero for everything.

Open the Health app → Sharing → Apps & Services → HealthyMama, and turn on Steps, Sleep and Heart Rate. iOS sometimes doesn't grant categories until you do this manually.

Can I share my journal with my partner or doctor?

Yes — Profile → Journal → Export Journal as PDF. You decide who sees it.
